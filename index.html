Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

BeadCanvas is an iOS app that turns photos into fuse-bead patterns. Photos, edits, and patterns stay on your device. We do not store your photos, patterns, or other personal content.

Your use of BeadCanvas is also covered by Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement. We do not publish a separate terms of use.

2. Information You Provide

You may choose a photo, edit it, and save a pattern or image to Photos. These files stay on your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

BeadCanvas does not ask you to sign in with an email or password.

3. Photos and Style Generation

Style generation uses the photo you selected to produce a result in the app. We do not store that photo, and we do not keep or use it afterward.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

BeadCanvas does not sell personal data. We do not collect contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

5. Anonymous Analytics

Anonymous analytics is on by default. You can turn it off in the Me tab. While it is on, the app reports usage events, such as which screens and tools you use. These events do not include your name, email, or photos.

This usage reporting is the information we collect. We do not otherwise store or use your content.

6. Purchases

Credit purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card number.

7. Children’s Privacy

BeadCanvas is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: chris_lin_95@foxmail.com
